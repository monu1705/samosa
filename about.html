About the project

ResumeSensei is a school-friendly prototype that demonstrates how AI-style resume guidance can feel — without any real AI required.

Why we built it

We wanted to show classmates how automated resume feedback might work, with complete transparency and no external services. Everything runs locally so it’s safe for a classroom setting.
